Rejected image and why:
I generated two hero background options in the identity kit's colors: (1)
a grid pattern with two soft translucent circles, and (2) a scattered
"network of dots" pattern connected by thin lines. I rejected option 2
because it read as busy and generic — it looked like a stock "tech/AI"
graphic rather than something tied to my actual identity, and it drew
attention toward itself instead of framing the work below it. Option 1
stayed calm and used the exact brand colors without competing with the
headline — matching the brief's own bar that design should frame the
work, never upstage it.

About page — no photo:
Chose not to include a personal photo. Kept the About page text-only so
the bio itself carries the "why is this person credible" answer, rather
than defaulting to a photo just because most portfolios have one.
