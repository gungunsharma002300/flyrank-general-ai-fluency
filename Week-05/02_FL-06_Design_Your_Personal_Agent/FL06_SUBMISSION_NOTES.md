# FL-06 Submission Notes

I designed a focused personal agent called **StudyPilot — Personal BCA Study Coach**.

Its single job is to help me study from my own notes by explaining topics, creating exam-style questions, running quizzes, making time-boxed revision plans, and tracking simple revision progress.

I chose a **scripted Python agent** because it is realistic to build in about 10 hours, keeps my notes local, and gives me direct control over retrieval, progress tracking, and guardrails.

The design includes a clear scope, realistic data/tool access, five pre-build evaluation cases, and guardrails for missing information and destructive actions.

The attached document is the agent design specification for FL-06.
