## 2. Reusable three-beat case-study structure

Every future case will follow the same Week 2 structure:

1. **Problem** — What real problem was being addressed?
2. **What I Did** — What was actually built/done, including the real tools, decisions, and AI assistance.
3. **What Came of It** — What real result, evidence, learning, or limitation came from the work.

## 3. Repeatable workflow

1. Verify the project before writing claims.
2. Collect real screenshots, links, repository/demo evidence, and test outputs where available.
3. Identify the specific problem rather than using generic wording.
4. Document my actual contribution and distinguish my work from AI assistance.
5. Record only verified outcomes; do not invent metrics, results, screenshots, or links.
6. Draft the case using **Problem → What I Did → What Came of It**.
7. Place the case inside the existing portfolio while preserving its established voice, stack, layout, and identity kit.
8. Review the page for consistency, working links, readable evidence, and a quick recruiter scan.
